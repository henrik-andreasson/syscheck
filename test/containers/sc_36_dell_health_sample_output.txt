### Key Fields Explained

* **Index**: The unique numeric ID for the specific sensor (used if you want to target a single probe with `omreport chassis temps index=n`).
* **Status**: The health state of the sensor (e.g., *Ok*, *Warning*, or *Critical*).
* **Probe Name**: The physical location or component being monitored (such as ambient air or CPU temperature).
* **Reading**: The current temperature measured in Celsius.
* **Warning Thresholds**: The low or high limits that trigger a warning event.
* **Failure Thresholds**: The critical limits that trigger hardware alerts or automatic thermal shutdown actions.
